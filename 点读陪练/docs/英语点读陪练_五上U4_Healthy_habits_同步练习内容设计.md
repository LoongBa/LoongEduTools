## 1. 三层句库

**词用范围**：
- 健康习惯：eat healthy food, drink enough water, exercise every day, get enough sleep, wash hands, brush teeth, rest eyes, eat breakfast
- 坏习惯：play phone in bed, watch TV too much, eat too much candy, don't exercise
- 句型：You should... / You shouldn't... / How can I stay healthy?
- 自然拼读：oa/ow（coat, play, slowly, goat, soap, goal, low, snow）

---

## 2. 能力目标
| 能力 | 检验任务 |
|---|---|
| 说该做什么 | You should eat healthy food. / You should get enough sleep. |
| 说不该做什么 | You shouldn't play phone in bed. / You shouldn't eat too much candy. |
| 说怎么保持健康 | How can I stay healthy? You should exercise every day. |

## 3. 七段路径
0 健康热身 / 1 健康习惯词大本营 / 2 You should... / 3 You shouldn't... / 4 健康清单 PBL / 5 oa/ow 语音 / 6 检验站。

## 4. 要点
- **句型**：You should + 健康习惯 / You shouldn't + 坏习惯
- **PBL**：健康习惯清单（一周打卡表，8 个好习惯打勾）
- **玩法**：好习惯/坏习惯分类 / 角色扮演（看医生）
