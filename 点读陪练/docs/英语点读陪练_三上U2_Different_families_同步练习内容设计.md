## 3. 单元知识骨架

| 知识点 | 原文 | 补全 | 扩展 | 落点 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 介绍家庭成员 | This is my grandma. / This is my grandpa. | 成员×This is my... ≈12 句 | This is my uncle. / This is my cousin. | 段 3 |
| 确认家庭成员 | Is this your brother? Yes, it is. / No, it's my cousin. | 成员×Is this your...? ≈10 句 | Is this your aunt? No, it's my mum. | 段 4 |
| 家庭大小 | My family is big. / I have a brother, a baby sister... | 大小×I have... ≈8 句 | I have two cousins. / My family is small. | 段 4 |
| 字母 Ee-Hh | egg/nest/fish/beef/girl/pig/hot/hat | 字母词×圈首音 ≈8 词 | E is for egg. / F is for fish. | 段 5 |
| PBL | Make a family tree（家庭树） | 树×成员填空 | 不同组合 | 段 4 |

## 4. 七段路径
0 家庭合影热身 / 1 家庭成员词大本营 / 2 This is my... / 3 Is this your...? / 4 家庭树 PBL / 5 字母 Ee-Hh / 6 检验站。

## 5. 分段要点
- **段 2 方法卡**："介绍 = This is my + 成员"
- **段 3 方法卡**："确认 = Is this your + 成员？→ Yes, it is. / No, it's my..."
- **段 4 PBL**：家庭树（三代：grandparents → parents → me/brother/sister）
- **段 5 字母**：Ee-Hh，圈首音，描红
