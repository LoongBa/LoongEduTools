## 二、每单元关键参数（生产用）

### U1 Helping at home
- **句型**：① What's your {who}'s job? He's a {job}. ② Can {subj} help? Yes, {subj} can. {subj} can {action}. ③ I can {action1} and {action2}.
- **槽表**：who=[father/grandpa/grandma/mother] / job=[farmer/nurse/doctor/office worker/factory worker] / action=[clean the room/sweep the floor/cook/do some chores]
- **语音 ch**：teach/Chinese/lunch/teacher/chair/child/peach/kitchen

### U2 My friends
- **句型**：① What's your friend's name? {His/Her} name is {name}. ② {He/She} is {adj}. {He/She} has {feature}. ③ {He/She} often {verb_s} with me.
- **槽表**：name=[Leo/Mia/Sam/Nana/Kiki/Bubu] / adj=[tall/strong/quiet/kind/funny/short/thin] / feature=[short hair/long hair/big eyes/small eyes/big ears]
- **语音 sh**：English/fish/share/she/ship/shop

### U3 Places we live in
- **句型**：① There is/are {place}. ② The {place} is {near/next to/behind} my home. ③ We often {activity} in the {place}.
- **槽表**：place=[park/shop/school/hospital/library/zoo/cinema/supermarket] / position=[near/next to/behind/in front of] / activity=[play/read/learn/watch films]
- **语音 ck**：back/black/duck/sock/tick/clock

### U4 Helping in the community
- **句型**：① He's a {job}. He {verb}s... ② He is {v-ing}... now.
- **语法重点**：进行时 v-ing（make→making 去 e 加 ing）
- **语音 ph**：phone/photo/elephant/Philip

### U5 The weather and us
- **句型**：① What's the weather like? It's {weather}. ② On {weather} days, we can {activity}.
- **槽表**：weather=[sunny/rainy/cloudy/windy/snowy/hot/cold] / activity=[fly a kite/make a snowman/go swimming/stay at home]
- **语音 th**：thank/these/there/them/think/they/three/thin

### U6 Seasons and clothes
- **句型**：① Whose {clothes} is this? It's mine/Mum's. ② In {season}, we wear {clothes}.
- **槽表**：season=[spring/summer/autumn/winter] / clothes=[sweater/jacket/coat/T-shirt/shorts]
- **语音 wh**：where/who/white/why/what/which/whose
