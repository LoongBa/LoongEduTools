## 5. 分段要点

### 段 1 · 农场动物
- **原文**：chickens / pigs / horses / cows / sheep
- **补全**：动物×点图 ≈10 句
- **玩法**：听叫声猜动物（moo/baa/oink/neigh/cluck）

### 段 2 · 问有多少动物
- **原文**：What animals do you have on the farm? / I have a lot of animals. / How many cows do you have? / I have 20.
- **补全**：动物×数量问答 ≈12 句
- **扩展**：How many sheep? / I have 15. / How many horses? / Ten.

### 段 3 · 这是什么/那些是什么
- **原文**：What are these? / They're tomatoes. / What are those? / They're bee boxes.
- **补全**：蔬菜/物品×远近问答 ≈15 句
- **扩展**：What are these? / They're carrots. / What are those? / They're potatoes.

### 段 4 · 我们为什么需要农场
- **原文**：Farms give us potatoes. / Farms give us tomatoes. / We all need farms to eat!
- **补全**：理由×Farms... ≈8 句
- **扩展**：Farms give us milk. / Farms give us eggs. / Farms give us meat.
- **方法卡**："农场的意义 = give us food + give us milk/eggs/meat + we need farms to eat"

### 段 5 · 从农场到餐桌（PBL · 3 步）
- **PBL 补全**：农场 → 超市 → 餐桌

### 段 6 · air 语音 + 能力检验
- **字母词**：chair/fair/hair/pair/air
- **玩法**：指读 air 词 → 圈首音
- **检验**：Self-check 4 条逐条过关
