## 5. 分段要点

### 段 1 · 描述外貌
- **原文**：Your dog is cute. It has a long body and short legs. / I love my rabbit. It has long ears and a short tail.
- **补全**：动物（dog/cat/rabbit/bird/fish/panda/monkey/elephant）× 形容词（long/short/fat/thin/slow/big/small/tall）≈15 句
- **扩展**：It has a big mouth. / It has short legs. / It's fat and slow.

### 段 2 · 问喜好
- **原文**：Do you like dogs? / Yes, I do. Dogs are friendly. They can help us too.
- **补全**：动物×问答 ≈10 句
- **扩展**：Do you like pandas? / Yes, I do. They're cute. / Do you like snakes? / No, I don't.

### 段 3 · 形容词词图配对
- **原文**：long / short / fat / thin / slow
- **补全**：形容词×反义词配对（long↔short, fat↔thin, fast↔slow）≈6 对
- **玩法**：点图发音 + 找朋友（反义词配对）

### 段 4 · 怎么表达爱
- **原文**：draw a picture / make a card / sing and dance / I often make her gifts.
- **补全**：方式×I... ≈10 句
- **扩展**：I draw a picture for Mum. / I sing and dance for Dad. / I make a card for my teacher.
- **方法卡**："表达爱四件事 = talk + draw pictures + sing songs + smile"

### 段 5 · 宠物朋友思维导图（PBL · 4 槽）
- **PBL 补全**：中心 My pet / 槽1 它是谁 / 槽2 它长什么样 / 槽3 我怎么爱它 / 槽4 我们一起做什么

### 段 6 · -e 语音 + 能力检验
- **字母词**：he/she/we/be/me/run/jump/dance
- **玩法**：指读 -e 词 → 圈首音 → 对比 he/hi, me/my
- **检验**：Self-check 4 条逐条过关
