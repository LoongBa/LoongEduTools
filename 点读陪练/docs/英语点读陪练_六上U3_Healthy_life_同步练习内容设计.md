## 1. 三层句库

**词用范围**：
- 疾病：cold, fever, cough, runny nose, headache
- 健康建议：drink water, have a rest, see a doctor, exercise, eat healthy food, sleep well, talk to friends
- 句型：What's the matter? I have a bad cold. / You should see a doctor. / You shouldn't stay up late.
- 健康生活三方面：body / mind / people

| 能力 | 检验任务 |

## 4. 要点
- **句型**：What's the matter? I have a + 疾病. / You should + 建议. / You shouldn't + 坏习惯.
