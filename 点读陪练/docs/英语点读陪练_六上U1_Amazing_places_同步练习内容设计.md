## 1. 三层句库

**词用范围**：
- 地标建筑：Great Wall, Palace Museum, Eiffel Tower, Sydney Opera House, Terracotta Warriors, West Lake
- 过去式动词：visited, climbed, went, ate, saw, took, walked, travelled, watched
- 句型：Where did you go? I went to... / What did you do? I climbed... / It was amazing.
- 地标形容词：long, tall, old, beautiful, famous
