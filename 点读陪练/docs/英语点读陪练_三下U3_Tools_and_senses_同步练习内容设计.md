## 5. 分段要点

### 段 1 · 借东西小剧场
- **原文**：Excuse me. Can I use your eraser, please? / Sure. Here you are. / Thanks. / I can't find my ruler. Can I use your ruler? / No problem.
- **补全**：物品（eraser/ruler/pencil/pen/book/bag/crayon）× 问答 ≈12 句
- **扩展**：Can I use your crayon? / Sure. Here you are. / Thanks. / You're welcome.

### 段 2 · 学习用品大本营
- **原文**：bag/book/pencil/pen/ruler/paper/eraser
- **补全**：物品×动作（Read/Write/Draw/Put）≈10 句
- **玩法**：点图发音 + 听指令做动作（Listen and do）

### 段 3 · 这是什么/这些是什么
- **原文**：What's this? / It's a pencil. / What are these? / They're grapes.
- **补全**：物品（单复数）× 问答 ≈12 句
- **扩展**：What's this? / It's a book. / What are these? / They're pens. / What are these? / They're apples.

### 段 4 · 五官小侦探
- **原文**：see/hear/smell/taste/touch / I smell with my nose. / I taste with my tongue.
- **补全**：五官×I... ≈10 句
- **扩展**：I see with my eyes. / I hear with my ears. / I touch with my hands.
- **方法卡**："五官 = see + hear + smell + taste + touch"

### 段 5 · 感官小书（PBL · 5 页）
- **PBL 补全**：封面 My sense book / 页1 I see... / 页2 I hear... / 页3 I smell... / 页4 I taste... / 页5 I touch...

### 段 6 · i-e 语音 + 能力检验
- **字母词**：kite/nine/nice/five/bike/like/fine/size
- **玩法**：指读 i-e 词 → 圈首音 → 对比 big/bike, sit/size
- **检验**：Self-check 4 条逐条过关
