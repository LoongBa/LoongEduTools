# 一上 Unit 3 Look, listen and learn 教材内容提取

> 来源：新课标：英语一年级上册（PEP全国版）.pdf 页 18-25

---

## 单元信息
- **单元名**：Look, listen and learn
- **核心问题**：What actions help us learn?（什么动作帮我们学习？）

---

## 1. Listen and chant（页 19）

```
I look at my teacher.
I listen and say.
I read my books.
I learn every day!
```

**Listen and sing**：Look, listen and learn

---

## 2. Listen, point and repeat（页 20）

**Classroom Instructions**（4 格动作图）：
- Look（指眼睛图）
- Listen（指耳朵图）
- Say（指嘴巴图）
- Read（看书图）

核心句：**Look! Look at me.**

**Listen and do**：
- Here is a picture. Look! Look! Look!
- Here is a song. Listen! Listen! Listen!
- Here is a book. Read! Read! Read!
- I read the book. My teachers say, "Good! Good!"

---

## 3. Listen and say（页 21）

- I show.（Binbin 拿卡片）
- I say.（Sarah）
- Look!（Binbin 举卡片）
- A book!（Sarah）

**Give a high five**：
- look / listen / say / read 四格打勾
- I look. I listen. I say. I read.
- Give me a high five!

---

## 4. Play a wheel game（页 22）

转盘游戏（6 格：Listen / Look / Read / Say / Show / Point）：
- In English class, we listen. We read. We ...
- We listen.（学生做动作）

---

## 5. PBL: Make a focus wall（页 23）

- **Step 1** Find these stickers.（找 4 张贴纸：耳朵/黑板/嘴巴/书）
- **Step 2** Make more stickers. Then stick them on the wall.
- **Step 3** Show your focus wall.（Focus wall 中心：learn English，四周 listen/look/say/read）

示范句：In my English class, I listen to my teacher. I say new words. I ...

---

## 6. Fun time 漫画（页 24-25，鹦鹉 Polly）

1. **盖着紫布**：Listen! / Wow!
2. **掀开布**：Look! Wow, a parrot! / This is Polly.
3. **教鹦鹉说话**：Polly, say hello! / Hello! Hello! / Wow!
4. **妈妈教**：John, say a word. Then listen.
5. **John 学**：Hi, Polly! / Hi, Polly! / Ha! Ha!（鹦鹉会说话了）
6. **John 谢妈妈**：Thank you, Mum. / Thank you, Mum.（鹦鹉也学）

---

## 核心词汇
- 学习动作：look, listen, say, read
- 课堂指令：show, point
- 其他：picture, song, book

## 核心句型
| 句型 | 用法 |
|---|---|
| Look! Look at me. | 看老师 |
| Listen! Listen to me. | 听老师 |
| I look. I listen. I say. I read. | 总结学习动作 |
| Give me a high five! | 击掌鼓励 |
