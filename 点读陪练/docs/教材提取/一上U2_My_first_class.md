# 一上 Unit 2 My first class 教材内容提取

> 来源：新课标：英语一年级上册（PEP全国版）.pdf 页 10-17

---

## 单元信息
- **单元名**：My first class
- **核心问题**：What do teachers say in class?（老师在课堂上说什么？）

---

## 1. Listen and chant（页 11）

```
Good morning, kids.
Good morning, Miss.
Hello, hello, hello.
Show me your book.
Show me your pencil.
I'm ready. Hooray!
```

**Listen and sing**：Let's begin!

---

## 2. Listen, point and repeat（页 12）

- A pencil!（Sarah 拿铅笔）
- A book!（Binbin 拿英语书）

**Listen and do**（动物指令）：
- Show me your pencil.
- Show me your book.
- Open your book.
- Close your book.
- Yes!

---

## 3. Listen and say（页 13）

**图 1（老师进教室）**：
- Good morning!（Miss White）
- Good morning!（学生）

**图 2（老师上课）**：
- Open your book, please.
- Now, show me your pencil.

**Role-play**：
- Good morning. I'm Miss White.
- Good morning, Miss White.
- Open your book, please. Show me your pencil, please.

---

## 4. Look and act（页 14）

（铅笔和书拟人化）：
- Hi! I'm a pencil.
- Hi! I'm a book.
- Good morning!
- Goodbye!

---

## 5. PBL: Make action cards（页 15）

- **Step 1** Get ready.（Pencil? Ready! Paper? Ready!）
- **Step 2** Make your action cards.（4 格动作图：open book / close book / show pencil / show book）
- **Step 3** Play with your partner.（Show me your pencil, please.）

---

## 6. Fun time 漫画（页 16-17，动物版课堂）

1. **猴子当老师**：Let's play "teacher and students". / OK!
2. **猴子发指令**：Show me your pencil, please!
3. **猴子发指令**：Open your book, please!
4. **猴子发指令**：Show me your paper, please!
5. **绵羊当老师**：Good... good morning, teacher. / Good morning.
6. **绵羊当老师**：Open your book, please!

---

## 核心词汇
- 文具：pencil, book
- 课堂指令：show, open, close
- 问候：good morning

## 核心句型
| 句型 | 用法 |
|---|---|
| Show me your pencil. | 指令：举给我看 |
| Open your book, please. | 指令：打开书 |
| Close your book. | 指令：合上书 |
| Good morning! | 早上问候 |
