# 一上 Unit 4 Ready for school 教材内容提取

> 来源：新课标：英语一年级上册（PEP全国版）.pdf 页 26-33

---

## 单元信息
- **单元名**：Ready for school
- **核心问题**：How do we get ready for school?（我们怎么准备上学？）

---

## 1. Listen and chant（页 27）

```
3, 2, 1. Ready? Go!
This is my bag.
This is my book.
Every morning, I go to school.

3, 2, 1. Ready? Go!
This is my cap.
This is my bottle.
Every morning, I go to school.
```

**Listen and sing**：Ready for school

---

## 2. Listen, point and repeat（页 28）

- Show me your bottle.（妈妈和 Sarah 在卧室收拾）

**Listen and do**（6 样物品指令）：
- Show me your book.
- Show me your pencil.
- Show me your pencil case.
- Show me your bag.
- Show me your bottle.
- And your cap!

---

## 3. Listen and say（页 29）

- This is my pencil case.（Sarah）
- This is my bottle.（John）

**Role-play**（鹦鹉 Polly）：
- Hi, Polly! This is my cap.
- Cap.（鹦鹉学）

---

## 4. Show and say（页 30）

**Check your school things**（5 样检查条）：
- 老师：This is my book.
- 学生1：This is my pencil.
- 学生2：This is my pencil case.
- 学生3：This is my bottle.

---

## 5. PBL: Make a tag for school things（页 31）

- **Step 1** Check your school things.（This is my book.）
- **Step 2** Make a tag.（做标签贴纸）
- **Step 3** Put the tag on your bag.（I'm ready!）

---

## 6. Fun time 漫画（页 32-33，收拾书包比赛）

1. **开始**：3, 2, 1. Ready? Go!（黑板写"收拾书包比赛"）
2. **装东西**：This is my pencil case. / This is my book.
3. **装东西**：This is my book. / This is my pencil case.
4. **装东西**：This is my cap. / This is my bottle.
5. **发现少了**：This is my bottle. / Oh no! My cap!
6. **找帽子**：I'm ready. / Oh, my cap!（帽子掉地上了）

---

## 核心词汇
- 书包物品（6）：bag, book, cap, bottle, pencil, pencil case
- 复现：pencil, book（U2 旧词）

## 核心句型
| 句型 | 用法 |
|---|---|
| Show me your bottle. | 指令：举给我看 |
| This is my + 物品. | 介绍自己的物品 |
| I'm ready! | 我准备好了 |
