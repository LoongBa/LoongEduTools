# 一上 Unit 5 People around me 教材内容提取

> 来源：新课标：英语一年级上册（PEP全国版）.pdf 页 34-41

---

## 单元信息
- **单元名**：People around me
- **核心问题**：Who are important to you?（谁对你重要？）

---

## 1. Listen and chant（页 35）

```
I love my mum. I love my dad.
They love me. They love me.
I love my sister. I love my brother.
They love me. They love me.
```

**Listen and sing**：My family

---

## 2. Listen, point and repeat（页 36）

- This is my brother.（运动会场景，指跑步的哥哥）

**Listen and sing**：
```
Who is he? Who is she?
He's my dad. She's my mum.
He's my brother. She's my sister.
They are all my family.
```

---

## 3. Listen and say（页 37）

**图 1（问同学）**：
- Who's he?
- He's Li Dong. He's my classmate.

**图 2（问家人）**：
- Who's she?
- She's my mum.

**Have a photo show**（照片秀）：
- Who's she? / She's my mum.
- Who's he? / He's my teacher.

---

## 4. Play a wheel game（页 38）

转盘游戏（6 格：mum / classmate / brother / sister / teacher / dad），中心 me：
- She's my mum. I love my mum.

---

## 5. PBL: Make a social map（页 39）

- **Step 1** Who are they? Tell a partner.（想 5 个家人/朋友）
- **Step 2** Choose Binbin or Sarah and make a social map.（以我为中心画社交关系图）
- **Step 3** Share your social map.
  - Who's he? / He's Binbin's dad.
  - Who's she? / She's Binbin's sister, Xinxin.

---

## 6. Fun time 漫画（页 40-41，送新年贺卡）

1. **做贺卡**：My New Year cards are ready.
2. **送哥哥**：This is for Sam. He is my brother.
3. **送爸妈**：This is for my dad. / This is for my mum.
4. **送老师**：This is for Miss White. She is my teacher.
5. **送同学**：This is for Chen Jie. She is my classmate. She is my friend too.
6. **送 Sarah**：This is for Sarah. She is my classmate. She is my friend too!

---

## 核心词汇
- 人物（6）：mum, dad, brother, sister, classmate, teacher
- 句型：Who's he? He's my... / Who's she? She's my...

## 核心句型
| 句型 | 用法 |
|---|---|
| Who's he? He's my dad. | 问他是谁 |
| Who's she? She's my mum. | 问她是谁 |
| This is my brother. | 介绍家人 |
