## 1. 三层句库

**词用范围**：
- 学科（8）：Chinese, maths, English, music, PE, art, science, IT
- 周末活动：do homework, tidy room, have pipa lessons, wash clothes, play football, play Chinese chess, cook lunch
- 句型：What do you have on Mondays? I have Chinese and maths. / I often play football.
- 频率副词：usually, often, sometimes
- 自然拼读：ie/igh（night, pie, lie, light, high, right, tight）

## 2. 能力目标
| 能力 | 检验任务 |
