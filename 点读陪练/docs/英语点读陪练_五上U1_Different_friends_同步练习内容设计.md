## 1. 三层句库

**词用范围**：
- 性格形容词：lovely, clever, young, hard-working, kind, friendly, quiet, strong, tall, short
- 能力动词：jump rope, play the piano, play Chinese chess, make robots, run fast, play football
- 句型：What's he/she like? He's/She's... / Can he/she...? Yes, he/she can. / Is he/she good at...?
- 自然拼读：ai/ay（day, play, rain, say, tail, train, wait, way）

---

## 2. 能力目标
| 能力 | 检验任务 |
|---|---|
| 说朋友性格 | He's tall and strong. / She's clever and lovely. |
| 说朋友能力 | He can jump rope. / She can play the piano. |
| 问他擅长吗 | Is he good at football? Yes, he is. |
| 猜谜描述朋友 | I have a friend. She's... She has... Can she...? |

## 3. 七段路径
0 新朋友热身 / 1 性格词大本营 / 2 What's he/she like? / 3 He/She can... / 4 朋友相册 PBL / 5 ai/ay 语音 / 6 检验站。

## 4. 要点
- **句型**：He's/She's + 形容词 / He/She can + 动词 / Is he/she good at...?
- **PBL**：朋友相册（填表格：性格+能力+擅长，配照片写介绍）
- **玩法**：猜谜游戏（描述朋友让别人猜是谁）
