## 5. 分段要点

### 段 3 · 职业+行为
- **原文**：He's a cleaner. He cleans the street. / She's a baker. She makes bread.
- **补全**：6 职业（cleaner/baker/farmer/doctor/driver/cook）× 行为 ≈24 句
- **扩展**：My dad is a driver. He drives a bus.

### 段 4 · 进行时（本单元核心新语法）
- **原文**：He is cleaning the street now. / She is making bread.
- **补全**：主语（He/She/They）× v-ing（cleaning/making/planting/helping/cooking）≈20 句
- **扩展**：They are planting trees. We are helping old people.
- **方法卡（语法点）**：**进行时口诀**——"正在做 = be + 动词 ing"；clean→cleaning, make→making（去 e 加 ing）, plant→planting。
- **玩法**：看图选句 + 进行时填空（He ___ (clean) the street now. → is cleaning）。

### 段 5 · 校园帮手海报（PBL · 4 槽）
- **PBL 补全（4 槽）**：I want to be a helper. / I help [场所]. / I help by [v-ing]. / I feel [happy/proud].
- **方法卡**："帮忙 = 在哪 + 做什么 + 感受"。

### 段 6 · ph 乐园
- **ph 词（6）**：phone, photo, elephant, Philip, photo, phrase
- **扩展（7）**：The elephant is big. / I have a phone. / Take a photo.
- **玩法**：听辨 / ph vs ch/sh/ck 归类（U1-U4 螺旋复习）/ 拼读 / 听写。

## 8. 复制验证发现
1. **新语法类型**：U4 = 进行时 be+doing → grammar_rule 加 "present_continuous"（v-ing 变位：去 e 加 ing）
2. **动词变位复杂度**：进行时 v-ing 比三单 s 复杂（make→making 去 e），方法卡要专做"去 e 加 ing"口诀
3. **语音跨单元复习**：ph vs ch/sh/ck（U1-U4）四拼归类
