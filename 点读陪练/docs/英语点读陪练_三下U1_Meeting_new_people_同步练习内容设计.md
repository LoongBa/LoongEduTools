## 5. 分段要点

### 段 1 · 打招呼
- **原文**：Hi! I'm Zhang Peng. What's your name? / My name's Amy Green. I'm new here.
- **补全**：名字（Amy/John/Mike/Sarah/Zoom/Zip/Chen Jie/Wu Binbin）× 问答 ≈12 句
- **扩展**：Hello! I'm Zoom. / What's your name? / My name's Zip. / Nice to meet you.

### 段 2 · 礼貌用语小剧场
- **原文**：Let me help. / Thank you. / After you! / You're welcome.
- **补全**：场景（开门/递东西/让座）× 对话 ≈8 句
- **扩展**：After you! / Thank you. / You're welcome. / Let me help you.
- **方法卡**："礼貌四件事 = Let me help + Thank you + After you + You're welcome"

### 段 3 · 你来自哪里
- **原文**：Where are you from? / I'm from the UK. / I'm from China.
- **补全**：国家（China/UK/Canada/USA）× 问答 ≈12 句
- **扩展**：I'm from Canada. / I'm from the USA. / We are friends. We like to play.
- **玩法**：国旗配对 + 点图发音

### 段 4 · 这是谁呀
- **原文**：Who's that girl? / That's our new neighbour, Amy. / That's my new neighbour, Mr Lin.
- **补全**：人物（boy/girl/woman/man/teacher/student/neighbour/classmate）× 问答 ≈10 句
- **扩展**：Who's that man? / That's my English teacher, Miss White. / He is from Canada.

### 段 5 · 国家朋友海报（PBL · 4 槽）
- **PBL 补全**：海报中心 Food festival / 槽1 人物名 / 槽2 来自哪国 / 槽3 礼貌用语 / 槽4 我能做什么

### 段 6 · a-e 语音 + 能力检验
- **字母词**：cake/name/make/game/Jake/Dave/date/hate
- **玩法**：指读 a-e 词 → 圈首音 → 对比 cat/cake, hat/hate
- **检验**：Self-check 4 条逐条过关
