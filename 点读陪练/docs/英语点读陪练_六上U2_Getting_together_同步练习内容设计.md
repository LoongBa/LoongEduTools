## 1. 三层句库

**词用范围**：
- 节日：Mid-Autumn Festival, Spring Festival, Dragon Boat Festival, Teachers' Day, Children's Day
- 节日活动：cleaned the house, pasted fu, dressed in red, waited for the gala, counted down, ate mooncakes, made zongzi
- 句型：Did you eat mooncakes? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. / What did you do?
- 大事件：marathon, book fair, sports meet, school trip

---

## 2. 能力目标
| 能力 | 检验任务 |
|---|---|
| 问节日做了什么 | Did you eat mooncakes? Yes, I did. / No, we didn't. |
| 说节日活动 | I cleaned the house. We pasted fu on the door. |
| 说大事件感受 | I liked the marathon. I cheered for the runners. |

## 3. 七段路径
0 节日热身 / 1 节日词大本营 / 2 Did you...? / 3 节日活动 / 4 学校活动照片 PBL / 5 不规则过去式复习 / 6 检验站。

## 4. 要点
- **句型**：Did you + 过去式...? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. / What did you do? I + 过去式.
- **PBL**：学校活动照片展（去年 vs 今年，写做了什么）
- **玩法**：节日猜谜（Did you count down? It's Spring Festival!）
