## 5. 分段要点

### 段 1 · 家规
- **原文**：Do my homework first. / Use kind words. / Tidy my room. / Wash my hands.
- **补全**：规则×祈使句 ≈12 句
- **扩展**：Don't run in the living room. / Don't jump on the bed. / Don't eat in bed.

### 段 2 · 家里房间
- **原文**：bedroom / study / living room / kitchen / bathroom
- **补全**：房间×问答 ≈10 句
- **玩法**：点图发音 + 找房间游戏

### 段 3 · 房间里的规则
- **原文**：Don't touch hot things. / Don't be so loud. / Don't play football in the living room.
- **补全**：房间×规则 ≈10 句
- **扩展**：Don't make a mess in the study. / Be quiet in the bedroom.

### 段 4 · 为什么有家规
- **原文**：This rule keeps me safe. / This rule keeps me healthy.
- **补全**：理由×This rule... ≈8 句
- **扩展**：Rules make us happy. / Rules think of others. / Rules keep us safe.
- **方法卡**："家规的理由 = safe + healthy + happy + think of others"

### 段 5 · 家规条约（PBL · 5 条）
- **PBL 补全**：全家同意的 5 条家规，签名

### 段 6 · er 语音 + 能力检验
- **字母词**：brother/dinner/river/water/under/after/computer/eraser
- **玩法**：指读 er 词 → 圈首音
- **检验**：Self-check 4 条逐条过关
