## 5. 分段要点

### 段 1 · 农场活动
- **原文**：plant potatoes / water carrots / pick tomatoes / feed chickens / milk cows
- **补全**：活动×名词 ≈12 句
- **扩展**：Farmers plant rice. / They water the flowers. / They pick apples.

### 段 2 · 餐具小课堂
- **原文**：knife / fork / chopsticks / bowl / spoon
- **补全**：餐具×使用 ≈10 句
- **玩法**：点图发音 + 配对（中西方餐具）

### 段 3 · 餐桌礼仪
- **原文**：Don't waste food. / Set the table. / Clear the table. / Don't play with your knife.
- **补全**：礼仪×祈使句 ≈10 句
- **扩展**：Don't point chopsticks at others. / Say "Thank you". / Help Mum clear the table.
- **方法卡**："餐桌礼仪 = set table + don't waste food + don't play with knife + clear table"

### 段 4 · 自己种西红柿
- **原文**：Spring is a good time to plant tomatoes. / Water every week. / Pick in summer.
- **补全**：步骤×I... ≈8 句
- **扩展**：I plant tomatoes in spring. / I water them every week. / I pick them in summer.

### 段 5 · 家庭农场日广告（PBL · 1 张）
- **PBL 补全**：农场日活动广告（做什么 + 吃什么）

### 段 6 · ear 语音 + 能力检验
- **字母词**：bear/dear/pear/year/hear/near/clear
- **玩法**：指读 ear 词 → 圈首音
- **检验**：Self-check 4 条逐条过关
