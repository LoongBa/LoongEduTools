## 1. 三层句库

**词用范围**：
- 能源：wind power, solar power, water power, green energy, electricity
- 用途：light, heat, cool homes, run cars, buses, trains
- 3R 规则：Reduce (use less), Reuse (bring own bags), Recycle (bottles, clothes, books)
- 句型：We use green energy to light our homes. / We should follow the 3R rules. / We can take quick showers.
