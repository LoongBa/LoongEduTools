## 1. 三层句库

- 钱相关：pocket money, piggy bank, save up, spend, sale, price, yuan
- 买什么：schoolbag, dictionary, basketball, gift, haircut, book
- 句型：How are you going to spend your pocket money? I'm going to buy... / I need... I want...
- 理财方法：write a spending plan, wait for a sale, save up, three jars (spend/share/save)
