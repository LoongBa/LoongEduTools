## 5. 分段要点

### 段 1 · 旧玩具
- **原文**：I have some old books. / That boat is cool. / You can keep it.
- **补全**：玩具（doll/ball/boat/car/book）× 问答 ≈10 句
- **扩展**：I have an old doll. / I have two old boats. / I have many old books.

### 段 2 · 东西在哪里
- **原文**：Where is my animal book? / Is it on the shelf? / No, it isn't. / It's in the box.
- **补全**：物品（book/cap/map/doll/ball/boat/car）× 位置（in/on/under）× 问答 ≈15 句
- **扩展**：Where is my map? / Is it on the desk? / No, it isn't. / It's under the bed.

### 段 3 · 位置词图配对
- **原文**：in / on / under
- **补全**：位置×物品配对 ≈6 对
- **玩法**：点图发音 + 找东西游戏

### 段 4 · 旧物利用小妙招
- **原文**：give away / sell / make new things
- **补全**：方式×I... ≈8 句
- **扩展**：I give old toys to my brother. / I sell old books. / I make new things from old boxes.
- **方法卡**："旧物利用三招 = give away + sell + make new things"

### 段 5 · 旧物利用项目（PBL · 3 分类）
- **PBL 补全**：箱1 送人 / 箱2 卖掉 / 箱3 改造

### 段 6 · u-e 语音 + 能力检验
- **字母词**：cute/use/tube/excuse/mule/note
- **玩法**：指读 u-e 词 → 圈首音 → 对比 cut/cute, us/use
- **检验**：Self-check 4 条逐条过关
