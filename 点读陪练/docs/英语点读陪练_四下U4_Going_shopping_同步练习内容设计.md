## 5. 分段要点

### 段 1 · 买衣服
- **原文**：My trousers are too short. / Can I buy a new pair? / Let's go to the clothes shop.
- **补全**：衣服（trousers/jacket/skirt/shorts/coat/T-shirt）× 请求 ≈12 句
- **扩展**：My shoes are too small. / Can I buy a new pair?

### 段 2 · 问价格小剧场
- **原文**：Can I help you? / Yes. I like this pink dress. / How much is it? / It's 180 yuan. / That's expensive. / OK. Let's take it.
- **补全**：物品×价格问答 ≈15 句
- **玩法**：店员/顾客角色扮演

### 段 3 · 衣服词图配对
- **原文**：trousers / jacket / skirt / shorts / shoes / hat / sunglasses
- **补全**：衣服×颜色（red/blue/black/white）≈10 句
- **玩法**：点图发音 + 颜色配对

### 段 4 · 聪明小买家
- **原文**：That's expensive. / That's cheap! / Try them on first.
- **补全**：价格×评价 ≈8 句
- **扩展**：It's beautiful! / That's too expensive. / Let's take the cheap one.
- **方法卡**："聪明购物三招 = 先看价格 + 再试穿 + 最后决定"

### 段 5 · 合唱比赛选服装（PBL · 3 套）
- **PBL 补全**：3 套服装选 1 套，说理由

### 段 6 · ir/ur 语音 + 能力检验
- **字母词**：girl/skirt/nurse/turn/bird/shirt/hurt/fur
- **玩法**：指读 ir/ur 词 → 圈首音
- **检验**：Self-check 4 条逐条过关
